        How it works

        Call Coach reads the conversation and shows two things: where it stands, on the gauge, and the one thing to do next.

        On a call

        1. Join your Zoom, Meet, Teams or phone call.
        2. Pick a coach, then press Auto capture to hear both sides.
        3. Glance at the gauge and do the suggested step.

        Practice

        1. Pick a practice coach and a scenario.
        2. Type or say your reply, then Get feedback.
        3. Try again to see the score move, or go to the next line.

        Your own coach

        1. Choose New live coach above, or duplicate one.
        2. Name the gauge's four levels and the next steps.
        3. Save. It's on the start screen right away.
