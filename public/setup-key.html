Call Coach

Welcome to Call Coach

A coach that floats over your calls and tells you what to do next.

  • Hears both sidesZoom, Meet, Teams or a phone app. Speech is turned into text on this computer.
  • Shows where things standA gauge that moves as the conversation does.
  • Suggests your next moveFor work calls, interviews, social situations, anxiety, or any coach you build.

Connect TypeSafe

Call Coach uses TypeSafe Jev to read the conversation. Paste your API key.

Don't have one? Get a key at typesafe.ai. Your key is saved only on this computer.

Choose a speech model

It runs on this computer and downloads the first time you use it. You can change it later in Settings.

You're set

Call Coach opens as a small window that stays on top of your call.
